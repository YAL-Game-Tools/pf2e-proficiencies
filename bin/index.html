YAL's PF2e Proficiency Table

This is a filterable table of class progressions in Pathfinder 2nd Edition!

Mode: pf2e · sf2e (WIP) · both

Remarks A rather non-exhaustive list.

Weapons

  • Fighters get better proficiencies in their chosen weapon group.
  • Gunslingers get better proficiencies in firearms and crossbows.
  • Clerics get better proficiencies in their deity weapons.

Class DC

  • Magi can use their Spell DC instead of Class DC on crits via Arcane Fists.
  • Clerics can use their Spell DC instead of Class DC on crits.

Saves

  • Monks pick their Master/Legendary saves.
  • Battle Harbingers don't get Will save degree upgrade despite getting Master.
